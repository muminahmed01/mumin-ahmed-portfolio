Frequently asked questions

How does a project start?

Message me on WhatsApp or use the inquiry form below. Share your goal and any examples you like, and I'll reply with a plan and a project-specific quote.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so I prepare a quote after we discuss your requirements. Use the "Get a Project Quote" button to start.

Can you guarantee AdSense or Adsterra approval, or Google rankings?

No. Approvals, earnings and search rankings are decided by the platforms and search engines. I prepare your website carefully and guide you through the setup, but I never promise guaranteed results.

Are your sample videos made with free or premium tools?

My portfolio samples were created with free tools. For client projects, I work with premium AI video tools, which give higher resolution and better quality, so the final result is stronger than the samples you see here.

How long does a project take?

Small jobs like an ad setup fix or a short video can be ready in a day or two. Website designs and larger video projects take longer, and I'll give you a clear timeline before I start.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. I want you to be happy with the result, so I refine the work until it matches what we agreed.
